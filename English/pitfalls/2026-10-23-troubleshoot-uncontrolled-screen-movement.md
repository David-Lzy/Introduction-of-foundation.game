# Troubleshoot a White Dot or Uncontrolled Screen Movement

If a white dot appears in the center of the screen, or the game view moves on its own, an external control device may be involved. Try these steps:

1. If you are not using a controller, disconnect any connected controllers and their wireless receivers, then restart the game.
2. Disconnect other peripherals too, such as joysticks or throttle controllers, and check whether the issue stops.
3. If it continues, share feedback in the [official Discord feedback channel](https://discord.com/channels/1275441685031157902/1467069508534534269).

This known-issue notice was published on October 22, 2026. It identifies peripherals as a possible cause; it does not confirm that they cause every instance of this behavior.
