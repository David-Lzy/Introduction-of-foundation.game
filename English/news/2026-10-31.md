# October 2026 Developer Feedback: migration planning, fairer guild matchmaking, and S2 access

The October developer feedback confirms several practical rules and previews upcoming changes. Dates and live eligibility can differ by server; check the in-game event pages before committing scarce resources.

- **Season 2 Magnetic Storm Resistance:** The team plans to add 300 total Resistance through certain buildings, allowing players to reach 6,000 without the Weekly Pass. Seasonal resources still reset at season end, so do not treat them as permanent stock.
- **Interstellar Migration:** The current target cadence is roughly every 3–4 months, but it is only an estimate and may vary by server. Each move requires **8 Migration Permits**, purchasable and savable in the Commerce Guild Shop. Inbound slots are fixed at **3/8/80/80** across four Power tiers. Check the live event page for actual server ratings, Power requirements, dates, and groups; not every rule will be announced beforehand.
- **Commerce Guild Duel League (from Nov 9):** Matchmaking will better account for guild strength, and Rank Points will reflect event-point performance more strongly. In Bronze–Gold, a strong performance may still earn a small number of points after a loss; Platinum and higher will see larger adjustments, including fewer losses for poor-performing defeats. Check the league rules after the update.
- **Duel recovery:** Stand Down rewards scale with eligible battle losses, with no stated maximum. When the recovery event appears, review its eligible-loss count and quests before claiming.
- **Event availability:** No additional time slots are currently planned for Defend the Trade Route or Aromabloom Trade War; no additional Shadowfront teams, participation slots, or sessions are planned either. A Shadowfront warm-up open to all Traders is under consideration, not confirmed.
- **Upcoming modes:** Amusement Park Defense is planned to join the weekly rotation, alternating with Kaboom, Robots! United Frontline is being tested for wider server availability. Dominion Warzone is set to add a Duel Day Chest capture-and-defend feature. No exact rollout dates were provided.
- **Unlock timing:** The fourth Flagship and Energy Core Levels 31–35 unlock on the Monday after a server reaches 27 weeks old. The fourth fleet requires Energy Core Level 33 and an available Flagship.
- **PC client:** The team says it is investigating update, startup, freezing, full-screen, and resource-use problems, but has not given a fix schedule.
