# 2026-11-17 Official hot update: Thanksgiving event and feature changes

The official notice lists a no-maintenance hot update for November 17; the list may change:

- A Thanksgiving Celebration is coming. Collect event items and join limited-time events for rare rewards; check in-game notices for dates and exact activities.
- The **Interstellar Migration** display period increases from 3 days to 5 days. Automated messages sent by Migration Officers when starting chats are also optimized.
- **Amusement Park Defense** gets a new theme that rotates with the existing one.
- The **Interrealm Expedition** stage in **Dominion Warzone** now ends at **23:30 UTC**.
- **Loot Chests** from **Commerce Guild Gifts** can stack up to **999**; the number of **Port Raid** messages that can be sent to the Commerce Guild channel is limited.
- The **Acting Prince** position is enhanced, and a new batch of portraits is available by default.
- Fixes address rallies being unexpectedly canceled when too many Flagships are near the target and a possible crash when folding some foldable phones; other optimizations and bug fixes are included.

Check the in-game version and notices to confirm when the event and changes are live.

Source: [Foundation official-account notice (Sotwe mirror)](https://www.sotwe.com/foundationgf_en?lang=en).
