# Nov 9, 2026 Update: Champions, Rewards Center, and Siwenna Balance

The update list may change; some features are limited to selected servers.

- **Teresa Lopez (Shadow Doctor)** is a limited Beam Defense Champion coming to Starry Recruitment. **Barbarian's Son**, a Legendary Beam Attack Champion, will be available through Champion Pass, Daily Purchase, and other methods. Both are for servers open at least 27 weeks; check the in-game availability before spending.
- Rewards for enemies defeated in Map Rallies move from Mailbox to **Rewards Center**. The Center also lists map chests available to collect, including Prince's Treasure and Sacred Tribute Vessel Chest.
- Minion search is capped at **three enemies per player**, regardless of level. Storage now has a Shop shortcut.
- Season 2 balance adjustments increase Electromagnetic Shield Tower Resistance, adjust first-Port-occupation Commerce Guild rewards, lower the difficulty of Lv. 1 Tribute Vessels, and optimize Ore Refinery upgrade costs and Magnetic Ore output. These changes are being tested on selected servers.

Source: [Official @FoundationGF_EN update post (readable mirror)](https://www.sotwe.com/FoundationGF_EN?lang=en).
