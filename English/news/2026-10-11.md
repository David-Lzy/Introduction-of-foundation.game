# Foundation Q4 2026 Roadmap: Events, Guilds, and Champions

The official Q4 development preview was posted on October 10. Everything below is in development or planning and may change; final timing, gameplay details, and availability will be confirmed in-game.

## Q4 preview

- **Guild Ruins:** Development will continue, expanding Ruin Raiders with more Commerce Guild collaboration.
- **Tower Defense:** Planned for the regular event rotation.
- **Premier Arena Tournament:** A cross-server team competition where top Traders represent participating servers. Other players can watch, cheer, and make predictions.
- **Guild Duel – War Prism Gameplay 2.0:** The battlefield is planned to use alternating attack and defense turns. More Commerce Guild members will be able to contribute in different ways and affect the result.
- **Item Exchange:** The initial plan supports exchanges for Flagship Component Fragments and Stellar Fragments, with a weekly exchange limit. Wait for the in-game rules before planning exchanges or spending fragments.
- **Two new Beam Champions:** These are planned to add team-building options.
- **Shadowfront League:** Paused for the quarter; next steps are not yet confirmed.

## What to do now

No launch dates, exchange ratios, numerical weekly caps, or tournament eligibility rules were announced. Do not spend fragments or assume an event is live based on this preview; adjust resources and Guild plans after official in-game details arrive.
