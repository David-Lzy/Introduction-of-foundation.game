# 2026-09-10 Epoch of Fusion Seed: prepare for Lvl 35 buildings and a fourth Battle Queue

- **Eligibility:** from Week 27 after a server launches, raise Combat Craft Modification to **60%** to unlock the Epoch of Fusion Seed technology tree at the Research Center.
- **First priority:** unlock and build the **Fusion Seed Refinery** immediately. Fusion Seeds pay for Epoch research and Lvl 31–35 building upgrades. Obtain them from the Refinery, Trader Commissions, Ruins Excavation, and related packs.
- **Scale production before spending:** the Refinery begins with one Fusion Reactor; related research adds two, and Refinery upgrades add three, for a maximum of six. Upgrade the existing reactors as well as expanding their count.
- **Milestones:** completing Fusion Power Systems raises the Energy Core cap to Lvl 35. Reaching **Energy Core Lvl 33** unlocks a fourth Battle Queue, so prioritize its prerequisite buildings and reserve Fusion Seeds rather than spreading them across secondary upgrades.
- **Fleet focus:** at **80%** Combat Craft Modification, Kinetic, Beam, and Ion Specialization trees unlock. Invest first in the tree matching your main fleet’s Style.
