# 2026-08-23 August 11 maintenance addendum: Core 33, Renewal Shop, and Duel Day preparation

## Check prerequisites before committing resources

- **Energy Core 33** unlocks the fourth battle queue. To obtain the **Fusion Seeds** needed for the upgrade, first reach the required Combat Craft Modification milestone to unlock the **Epoch of Fusion Seed** technology and **Fusion Seed Refinery**. This Energy Core expansion was initially testing only on Servers **1001–1016**; do not commit resources unless the feature is visible in-game.
- **Kinetic Mastery**, **Beam Mastery**, and **Ion Mastery** are gated by the same Combat Craft Modification milestone. Choose the primary style before investing in its technology and buildings instead of spreading resources too thinly.
- The **Renewal Shop** is available only on servers open for at least **five weeks**. Excess Flagship Components, Flagship Blueprints, and Oath Crystals can be converted for shop items; confirm that the shop is present and compare its offers before converting scarce inventory.

## Current event and convenience changes

- Commerce Guild Duel Days now award points based on each player's Combat Craft destroyed. Before deploying, weigh attainable enemy craft kills against the losses you can afford.
- The next **Interstellar Migration** batch covers Servers **1121–1152**. Verify that your server and its window are eligible in-game before preparing.
- Custom Flagship names are available, and Windows/Mac clients add keyboard shortcuts. Availability should always be confirmed from the live in-game client.
