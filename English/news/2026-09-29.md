# September 2026 Developer Feedback: practical changes and planning notes

The September 2026 developer feedback answers 11 player questions. The following points affect character creation, Season 2 planning, upgrades, and event preparation:

- **Create a character before the server enters Season 2 (S2).** New characters cannot be created on that server after it enters S2.
- **Plan for a one-time S2.** S2 will run once per server. When it ends, players remain on the **Siwenna** map and return to regular gameplay. Magnetic Storm content and buildings that provide Resistance reset at season end; treat them as seasonal rather than permanent progression.
- **Lily fragments:** the monthly purchase limit in the **Valor Shop** increased from 20 to 40. Check the shop’s current stock and use the higher limit if you are developing Lily.
- **Commerce Guild Duel recovery:** a recovery period for repairs and resource replenishment is planned, but its timing has not been announced. Do not schedule around a specific date yet.
- **Interstellar Migration:** some groups have expanded to 16 servers, but available destinations vary by progression stage. Check the in-game server list for your group’s actual options.
- **Quick Glory Shop access:** tap the resource bar to open the Glory Shop directly.
- **Report suspected event bots** to Customer Support with the relevant details so they can investigate.
- A new **Champion** is expected in late October 2026; role, abilities, and lineup details were not yet announced as of September 28.
