# 2026-09-11 Season 2 FAQ: protect your transition plan before launch

- **Who starts and when:** Season 2 is scheduled to begin on **September 14, 2026** for **Servers 1–16**. Other servers must be open for at least **90 days** and wait for post-launch stability validation; their date is not yet announced.
- **Before login:** finish or claim what you can from Season 1 main-story missions, Strikeback, and exploration. Logging in moves you automatically to **Siwenna**, where unfinished Season 1 content cannot continue; the FAQ says retroactive rewards will be issued for the affected progress.
- **Coordinate your Commerce Guild:** membership, research progress, and Guild level carry over. Members may land in different starting regions, so regroup through connected outer regions or teleport to the Guild Hub after the Guild Leader establishes it.
- **Do not plan around a merge:** every server receives its own Siwenna map; the four starting regions are areas within that map, not separate servers. Later servers start Season 2 from Phase 1 independently.
- **Spend seasonal materials in-season:** Resistance Crystals and Magnetic Ore begin from zero, do not replace Water or Metal, and unused amounts are removed at season end. Interstellar Migration is suspended during Season 2 and cannot operate between Season 1 and Season 2 servers.
