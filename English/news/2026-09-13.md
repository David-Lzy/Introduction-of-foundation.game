# Season 2 schedule and launch maintenance (Servers 1001–1016)

- **Use the in-game calendar from Season 2 onward.** The official account says social-media calendars will stop because schedules differ by server. This notice applies to **Servers 1001–1016 from 14 September 2026**.
- **Plan around the first weeks:** Top 100 Galactic Traders runs in Weeks 1, 2, and 4 only; Commerce Guild Duel first opens in Week 3; Dominion Warzone first opens in Week 6. Pathbreaker Operation and Aromabloom Trade War unlock later as the season progresses.
- **Launch maintenance:** Server 1001 is scheduled to be offline 13 Sep 23:30–14 Sep 06:00 UTC; Servers 1002–1016, 13 Sep 23:30–14 Sep 07:00 UTC; all other servers, 14 Sep 02:00–07:00 UTC. Do not schedule time-sensitive actions across your server's window. The Usurper is listed for testing on Servers 1001–1016.
