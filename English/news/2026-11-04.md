# Dominion Warzone: Battlefield Rules and Loss Recovery (Nov 4, 2026)

The official account added details on fleet activity, combat-loss recovery, and Duel Day Chests. Check the in-game rules for the version available on your server.

- **Fleet activity:** Once cross-server combat begins, fleets inactive for too long without Auto Rally enabled are temporarily hidden. Attacking fleets left idle for an extended period automatically return to their home server. Stay active during the battle and enable Auto Rally when appropriate.
- **Day 6 loss recovery:** Combat Craft losses from battles against players on other servers are tracked. After Battle Day, **30%** of the corresponding resources and Shipbuilding Speedups are returned. Claim the recovery rewards within **3 days**.
- **Duel Day Chests:** Chests spawn around the Ascendancy Fortress the first time occupation progress reaches **10%, 25%, and 50%**. Attackers deliver a chest to the Jump Point to earn one Duel Day Chest for their server; defenders earn one by defeating an attacking Flagship carrying a chest. At settlement, the winning side receives **20 additional chests**.
