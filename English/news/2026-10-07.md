# Interstellar Migration: Servers 1113–1192 (October 13–19, 2026)

A new Interstellar Migration round is scheduled for Servers 1113–1192 ahead of Season 2. Each group contains 16 servers:

- 1113–1128
- 1129–1144
- 1145–1160
- 1161–1176
- 1177–1192

## Schedule (UTC dates)

- **Oct 13:** Migration opens; announcement period begins.
- **Oct 13–15:** Migration announcement period.
- **Oct 16–17:** Applications.
- **Oct 18:** Migration Day.
- **Oct 19:** Migration display period.

Plan around the in-game schedule for your server. The announcement says this will be the last migration opportunity for these servers before Season 2; it does **not** mean S2 will launch immediately afterward, and no S2 date for other servers is confirmed. Do not estimate Power Tiers from previous migrations, as that may lead to incorrect planning.
