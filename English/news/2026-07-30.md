# 2026-07-30 Official update: Flagship cap, Potent Empowerment, and limited-time community events

## Key changes from the July 28 maintenance

- The **Commerce Guild Duel League** test has ended. Test-season rewards were mailed to all Commerce Guild members; the regular **six-month** league season begins with the next Commerce Guild Duel.
- The **Flagship Component** level cap is now **70**, available only on servers open for at least **27 weeks**.
- Flagship Component interfaces were improved, including **Quick Craft** for Component Blueprints.
- **Potent Empowerment** unlocks when the matching Flagship Component reaches **Empowerment +6**. It consumes the new **Catalyst Device** and lets players spend multiple **Prismatic Cores** in one Empowerment attempt for more Luck.
- **Dominion Warzone** now supports bye rounds. Players on a server receiving a bye can still participate during the event's first five days and claim the corresponding rewards.
- Commerce Guild Executive Managers can once again cancel rally deployments started by members during cross-server battles.

## What players and Commerce Guilds should do

- Servers approaching 27 weeks should prepare materials for Level 70 Flagship Components. On younger servers, the missing cap is expected rather than a bug.
- Before pushing core components to Empowerment +6, plan Catalyst Device and Prismatic Core reserves.
- Treat the next Commerce Guild Duel as the start of a long season, not another short test. Officers should plan attendance, retention, and resource allocation around six months.
- If your server receives a Dominion Warzone bye, do not skip the first five days; complete the available content and claim rewards.

## Spaceship-design voting (July 28-August 3)

- Each player may vote for up to **five submissions**.
- The most-voted design will be implemented in-game and given to the community for free.
- 🎮 marks entries eligible for implementation; AI-marked entries are prize-only and cannot be implemented.
- Use the Google Forms link in the official post to vote.

## Murdoku Challenge (ends August 5 at 23:59 UTC)

1. Use each suspect's clue to place them on the grid.
2. Only one person may occupy each row and column. Beds may be occupied; object squares such as TVs, apples, and tables may not.
3. After placing a character, eliminate every other square in that row and column.
4. Riian occupies the final empty square; the suspect alone with Riian in the same area is the killer.
5. Reply to the official post with the killer's name and your UID. Ten randomly selected correct entries each receive **1,000 Credits**.

> The post and puzzle image must be used together. The text clues alone do not support a reliable unique answer, so this guide does not publish an unverified solution.
