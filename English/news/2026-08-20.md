# 2026-08-20 August 11 maintenance: Energy Core expansion and battle-queue preparation

## Test-server only: confirm eligibility before committing resources

- This **Energy Core Level Expansion** is being tested on Servers **1001–1016**. Use this page only if your server is in that range and the feature is visible in-game; the announcement says the list remains subject to change.
- The new **Epoch of Fusion Seed** technology unlocks the **Fusion Seed Refinery** and increases **Fusion Seed** production. Once unlocked, buildings can reach **Level 35**, with related limits raised alongside the Energy Core cap.
- A fourth battle queue unlocks at **Energy Core Level 33**, allowing up to four Flagships to deploy at once. Before pushing for that threshold, prepare a fourth independently usable fleet so the extra queue is immediately useful.

## Directly useful changes in this update

- **Kinetic Mastery**, **Beam Mastery**, and **Ion Mastery** unlock after Combat Craft Modification reaches its required milestone. Treat current modification progress as a prerequisite rather than assuming these trees are available immediately.
- The new **Renewal Shop** is available only on servers open for at least **five weeks**; it adds Flagship Components, excess Flagship Blueprints, and Oath Crystals to the conversion system. Confirm server age and the shop entry before clearing inventory.
- **Interstellar Migration** is planned for Servers **1121–1152**. Confirm that your server belongs to this batch before preparing for its migration window.
