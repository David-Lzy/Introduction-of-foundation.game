# 2026-08-08 Google Play update: Champion weapons and live Commerce Guild Duel League

## Champion-exclusive weapons: server-age gate and preparation

- Champion-exclusive weapons were added for **Fist of Luminism — Aliya** and **Warden — Eva von Trier**.
- They are available only on servers that have been open for **at least 27 weeks**. If the feature is absent on a younger server, it is an eligibility gate rather than a missing unlock.
- Before investing fragments, check the updated fragment description: it now states the compatible Champion role and Style. Match both fields before committing materials.

## Commerce Guild Duel: live League window

- The **Commerce Guild Duel League** is officially live. Google Play lists the current “Rise Through the Ranks” event as ending on **August 17** (local storefront date).
- Rally Commerce Guild members before each match and treat the remaining window as an active competitive period, not a pre-season test.
- In Duel Day, attacking a **War Prism** now automatically removes **Interstellar Shelter**. Officers should confirm whether shelter is part of a target plan before assigning the attack.
