# 2026-08-29 August 25 maintenance: Arena settlement and cross-server preparation

## Check server eligibility before planning resources and lineups

- Energy Core expansion features, including Fusion Seeds and the fourth battle queue, are available on servers open for at least **27 weeks**. Confirm the feature is visible in-game on your server before planning Core, technology, or battle-queue resources.
- You cannot start a new **Glory Arena** battle during the **15 minutes** before settlement. Schedule your last ranking attempt before the lockout. Defensive lineups now carry into the next Arena season automatically, so check and adjust your defense immediately after settlement.
- The cross-server version of **Ruins Raiders** is testing on Servers **1001–1016**. Players in that range should confirm the in-game entry and matching rules before spending event resources.

## Avoid unnecessary losses in occupation and progression

- After defeating a garrisoned fleet and occupying a Fortress or Planet, the highest-Power Flagship among the occupying members is automatically selected as Leader. Agree on the intended occupier before rallying so the wrong Flagship does not receive the leadership position.
- Speedup use now consumes the matching category first, then Universal Speedups. When resources are short, the replenishment screen can quickly open the required number of Choice Chests and Level Chests. Review the consumption preview before confirming, especially if Universal Speedups are reserved for an event.
- An optimized **Starry Recruitment** is in testing: cumulative recruitment rewards were adjusted and event packs now refresh daily. Commit to concentrated recruitment only after the event panel is live on your server and its current reward table is visible.
