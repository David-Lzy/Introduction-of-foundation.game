# Season 2: Siwenna launch schedule (Servers 1113–1192)

- **November 16–22, 2026 (UTC):** Season 2 preview period.
- **November 23, 2026 (UTC):** Season 2 launches for Servers **1113–1192**.
- If your server is in this range, use the preview week to review the new season information and coordinate with your Commerce Guild and fleet before launch. Confirm the schedule in-game in case it changes.
