# October 15 Update: New Events and Guild Tools

The October 15 update announcement adds several items worth checking in-game. Availability may depend on server and event schedule.

- **Commerce Guild Duel:** A new Sunday “Stand Down” phase lets players complete quests for rewards based on their guild’s combat losses from Duel Day. Check the phase’s quests and reward conditions when it appears.
- **Amusement Park Defense:** This ground-combat event will alternate with “Kaboom, robots!” on Sundays and Mondays. Its initial test rollout is limited to Servers 1001–1016; check the event panel to confirm eligibility and schedule.
- **Season 2 Dominion Warzone:** The announced format uses matchups and elimination rounds, with seasonal server rankings. Detailed mechanics and rewards have not yet been published; review the in-game rules before committing resources.
- **Guild and event QoL:** Guild Member search supports member names, and Champion Weapons gain a rankings view. In Season 2 AromaBloom Trade War, chest-analysis progress from some Trader Commissions is doubled; qualifying commissions were not specified, so check the live event objectives.

The announcement also previews the Astral Alignment Party anniversary celebrations but does not yet give event dates or reward details.

Source: [Murpo briefing with links to the official Foundation Discord announcement](https://murpo.com/news/klara-patch-notes-e260907235808ba90080) (October 14, 2026).
