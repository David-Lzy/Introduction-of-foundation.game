# Season 2: Siwenna launch schedule (Servers 1017–1112)

Season 2 launches on **November 9, 2026 (UTC)** for **Servers 1017–1112**. Dates for other server groups have not been announced; do not infer your server’s launch date from this batch.

- **November 2–8:** Pre-download season resources and view the Season 2 preview.
- **November 5–8:** Check your season group in-game and use it to coordinate preparations with your Commerce Guild.
- **November 9:** Season 2 opens on the servers listed above; set course for Siwenna.

Check your in-game server notices while preparing. The announcement says dates for more servers will follow.

Source: [Murpo briefing linking the official Foundation Discord announcement](https://murpo.com/news/klara-game-news-b3769f105bb90f5cb1f3) (October 28, 2026).
