# Known Issues on Nov 1: Commerce Guild Duel Rewards, Flagship Movement, and In-Game Mail

The official notice lists three issues under investigation: some players cannot claim Commerce Guild Duel rewards; some Flagships become stuck and cannot move; and all in-game mail may disappear after logging back in. No workaround or fix schedule was provided. If affected, watch for follow-up official notices; do not assume unclaimed rewards or mail contents are safely retained.

Source: [Murpo reproduction of the official notice, with a link to the original Discord post](https://murpo.com/news/klara-known-issues-c693bcc378f25d2e4fa8).
