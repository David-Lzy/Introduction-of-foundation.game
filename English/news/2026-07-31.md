# 2026-07-31 Official Developer Feedback: Season 2 pilot, migration handling, and recycling

## Confirmed rollout and eligibility changes

- **Season 2** has completed development and is in large-scale testing. The first August rollout is planned for **Servers 1001–1016**; wider rollout timing depends on feedback from that pilot.
- On **August 10**, **Dominion Warzone** eligibility expands so every server open for more than **nine weeks** can participate.
- **Item Recycling** launches on **August 11**. On servers open for more than **30 days**, the inventory shows recycling for eligible items. The first **Recycling Coin** unlocks the **Recycling Store**; the eligible-item and store-reward lists will be published in-game.

## Migration and combat decisions

- Interstellar Migration has no stated outbound limit, but destination slots are capped by power tier. Confirm the destination's live inbound slots before committing resources or applying.
- If the **Trader Prince** is unavailable and migration applications are blocked, contact Customer Support. After verification, the team may appoint an eligible Trader as **Migration Officer**.
- For **Commerce Guild Duel**, **Dominion Warzone**, **Shadowfront**, and Cross-Server Arena, the matchmaking pool has been expanded. Treat the next event round as the first test of the revised grouping, rather than assuming prior matchups will repeat.
- PvP Glory comes primarily from effective damage against Traders outside your Commerce Guild. Do not enter with a nearly destroyed Flagship or without Combat Craft against a full Combat Craft formation: the official rules identify those as cases where Glory may not be awarded.

## Practical checklist

1. If your server is 1001–1016, watch the in-game Season 2 notice and report pilot-specific changes before treating them as global rules.
2. For a server nearing nine weeks, schedule Dominion Warzone preparation for the next eligible window after August 10.
3. Before migration, verify your target server's tier and inbound capacity; escalate a stalled approval chain to Support instead of waiting for an offline Trader Prince.
4. Keep potentially recyclable inventory items until August 11, then inspect the live eligible-item list before dismantling anything.
5. In cross-Commerce Guild PvP, repair your Flagship and field Combat Craft before chasing Glory.
