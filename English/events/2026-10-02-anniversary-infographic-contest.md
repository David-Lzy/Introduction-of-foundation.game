# Anniversary Infographic Guide Contest: Solo or Duo Entry

Foundation: Galactic Frontier is holding an anniversary infographic guide contest. Choose any game mechanic, strategy, system, or tip and explain it in a clear, easy-to-scan infographic. You may use the supplied template or your own. Enter solo or with one teammate (maximum team size: two).

- **Submission window:** October 1 through October 19, 2026, 12:00 UTC.
- **Voting window:** October 20 through October 26, 2026, 00:00 UTC.
- **How to enter:** Pick a game topic → create a concise visual guide → submit it through the channel specified in the official announcement and follow its complete rules → participate during the voting window.
- **Note:** The topic is open-ended. Check the original official Discord post for submission requirements, channel, and voting rules.

Sources: [Official Foundation Discord announcement](https://discord.com/channels/1275441685031157902/1275454288507961438/1555149809101574176); [announcement summary](https://murpo.com/news/klara-community-news-6b2aedb8030a6dcab171).
