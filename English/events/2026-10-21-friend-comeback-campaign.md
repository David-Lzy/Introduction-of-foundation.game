# Friend Comeback Campaign: Invitation and Return Missions

The campaign page is available through **November 30, 2026**. All players can complete Invitation Missions; eligible returning players can also take part in Return Missions. Both the inviter and returning player receive rewards for completed missions. Completing both mission tracks can award up to **50 Flagship Blueprint Selection Boxes** and **10 Interstellar Voyage Memories** in total.

**What to do:** Open the campaign page and complete the Invitation Missions. If your account meets the return criteria, complete the Return Missions as well. Check the in-game campaign page for eligibility and exact tasks.

Source: [FunPlus press release (PR TIMES)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000002.000193102.html) (October 21, 2026).
