# Astral Alignment Party — Anniversary Event Guide

The official-account post mirrored by Sotwe lists these event windows:

- **Servers 1001–1016:** October 16–November 4, 2026.
- **Servers 1017–1264:** October 19–November 7, 2026.

## What to do

- **Anniversary Sign-In:** Log in on any three days during the event; the days do not need to be consecutive. The announced reward is the Darkstar Core ship skin.
- **Anniversary Treasure Hunt:** Explore limited-time ruins and use the additional daily excavation and plunder attempts while the event is active.
- **Channel Calibrator:** Spend Calibration Keys to earn rewards, reach milestone prizes, and try the Jackpot Wheel.
- **Port Parade Race:** Complete quests, improve your ship, and compete for ranking rewards across 18 parade stops.
- **Reignite the Stars:** Team up with other Traders to restore planets, unlock milestone rewards, and work toward the celebration planet.

## Reignite the Stars: cooperative and solo construction

Use Construction Components to advance construction stages and claim rewards as planets are restored. Team up with other Traders to restore all four planets and unlock the Alignment Celebration Planet; after that, continue construction on your own. The event runs October 16–21 on Servers 1001–1016 and October 19–24 on Servers 1017–1264. Check the in-game event page for your server.

## 650K anniversary community milestones

The official account is running a combined follower/member campaign across its official social platforms and Discord through **November 16, 2026, 23:59 UTC**. Each newly reached milestone releases a redemption code; claim it in-game when announced. The announced reward tiers are:

- **600K:** 4 Virtuous Memories, 40,000 Galactic Coins ×2, and 30-minute Speedups ×2.
- **620K:** 6 Virtuous Memories, 40,000 Galactic Coins ×3, and 30-minute Speedups ×3.
- **650K:** 10 Virtuous Memories, 40,000 Galactic Coins ×10, and 30-minute Speedups ×10.

The code is released only when a new milestone is reached; the post does not provide a code in advance.

## Boom-Bang Bot Bash test

The new Tower Defense feature is being tested on **Servers 1001–1016 on October 18–19, 2026**. Deploy and upgrade Defense Towers, choose buffs for Champions, and defeat waves of enemies with area attacks. Complete Normal Mode to unlock Endless Challenge. This is a limited test window; check the in-game event page for availability.
